Who's trending, and what should I do about it?

Built from every player's weekly role: snaps, targets, carries, red-zone chances and share of his team's targets. Opportunities = targets + carries (pass attempts + carries for QBs). Expected points (xFP) = what that role is usually worth in this league's scoring, fit on this season and last; a player scoring well above it is running hot (often touchdowns), well below it is running cold. Role changes stick far more often than hot or cold streaks.

Sell high
